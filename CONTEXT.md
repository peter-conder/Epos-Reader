# Epos Reader

A reader for the great classical epics (Vergil's Aeneid and Homer's Odyssey and Iliad) in the original language, with scansion, word-by-word grammar and translation. It is for students, self-taught readers and its author's own study.

## Language

### The texts

**Epos Reader**:
The product. Always the full two-word name.
_Avoid_: Epos (on its own, since EPOS is a common acronym), Epos-Reader (that's the repo name)

**Work**:
One epic poem: the Aeneid, the Odyssey or the Iliad.
_Avoid_: Poem, text, title

**Book**:
One of the numbered divisions of a Work, written in Roman numerals (Aeneid I–XII, Homer I–XXIV).
_Avoid_: Chapter, canto, section

### Reading

**Word card**:
The panel that shows one word's lemma, grammar and definition when the reader picks the word.
_Avoid_: Gloss, popup, tooltip

**Split**:
Two text tabs shown side by side, each with its own Work and Book.
_Avoid_: Dual view, dual pane, pair

**Parallel translation**:
The English translation shown next to the original, line by line.
_Avoid_: Dual view, dual pane, English pane
