# Bookmarks on phones and tablets, Kindle-style

Agreed in a design review on 2026-11-25.

- **Corner ribbon** (phones and tablets, Latin only): a Kindle-style ribbon tab hanging from just under the status bar at the top right. Empty, it is an outline that fades with the menu button and bottom bar; filled (any Bookmarked Line on screen) it is solid and stays while you read. Tapping an empty ribbon bookmarks the first Line you can read at the top of the screen; tapping a filled one removes the topmost Bookmark on screen. These are ordinary Line Bookmarks.
- **Dots**: a strip down the right edge, one dot per Bookmark at its place in the Book, shown and faded with the controls, 44px targets. Tapping one puts its Line at the top of the screen and flashes it.
- **Back to line N**: after a dot jump, a chip above the bottom bar returns to where you were. It goes when tapped, after scrolling more than a screen, after 8 seconds, or on another jump.
- Line-number taps, the long-press menu and the word card's button are unchanged. No ribbon or dots while the English is swapped in, or on the Notebook and Home. Desktop is unchanged.
